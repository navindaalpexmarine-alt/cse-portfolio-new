Contact

Feedback, bug reports, feature ideas and privacy questions are welcome.

How to reach us

Email: YOUR_EMAIL@example.com

Please include a short subject line (e.g. “Bug: prices not updating” or “Privacy request”) so we can respond faster.

What to include for bug reports

  • Device and browser (e.g. Android Chrome, iPhone Safari, desktop Firefox)
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened (screenshot if useful)
  • Approximate time of the issue

Privacy or account requests

For access, correction or deletion requests related to your account data, email us from the same address you used to register. You can also delete your account yourself from Settings → Delete account inside the app.

Response time

This is a small project. We aim to reply within a few days, but there is no guaranteed support SLA.
