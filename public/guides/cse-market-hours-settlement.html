CSE market hours, holidays and T+2 settlement

The Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) trades on weekdays except holidays, with a short pre-open session followed by regular trading until 2:30 pm Sri Lanka time. Trades settle two market days later. These timings decide when you can trade, when your money moves, and whether you qualify for a dividend.

Not investment advice. Times are as published by the CSE at the time of writing. The CSE can change them or delay the open; check its notices.

The trading day

Time (Sri Lanka)SessionWhat happens
9:00 – 9:30 amPre-openOrders can be entered, amended and cancelled. Nothing trades yet.
9:30 amOpen auctionThe system calculates an opening price for each share from the pre-open orders and matches them.
9:30 am – 2:30 pmRegular tradingContinuous matching: orders trade whenever a buy price meets a sell price.
2:30 pmCloseRegular trading ends. Closing prices and the day's index values are published.

Trading hours have changed several times in recent years (for example during 2020 and 2022), so if you return after a break, check the current schedule. On rare days the CSE extends the pre-open or delays the open, as happened on one day in July 2026 when trading began at 11:00 am after a technical issue with CDS accounts.

How the open auction works

During pre-open, buy and sell orders build up without matching. At 9:30 am the system finds the single price at which the largest number of shares can trade and executes matching orders at that price. This avoids a chaotic first minute and gives a fair opening price, especially after overnight news.

Orders during regular trading

  • Limit order: buy at or below, or sell at or above, a price you set. Most retail orders are limit orders.
  • Market order: trade at the best available price now. Fast, but on thinly traded shares the price can be far from the last trade.
  • Validity: an order can be for the day or remain open for a set period, depending on your broker's platform.

Prices move in fixed steps called ticks, so you cannot place an order at any price you like; the platform will round or reject prices that are not on a valid tick.

T+2 settlement

Since 10 June 2024 equity trades on the CSE settle on T+2: shares and money change hands on the second market day after the trade date (T). Before that, settlement was T+3, in place since 2007.

Trade day (T)Settlement (T+2)
MondayWednesday
ThursdayMonday
FridayTuesday
Day before a holidayMoves forward by the number of market holidays in between

For buyers, your broker needs your money by its deadline so it can pay on settlement day. For sellers, the shares leave your CDS account and the sale proceeds become available after settlement, then reach you according to your broker's payment process.

Why settlement matters for dividends

To receive a dividend you must be on the shareholder record. The XD date is set with settlement in mind, so buying before the XD date gets you the dividend and buying on or after it does not. Details in dividends on the CSE.

Holidays

The CSE is closed on Saturdays and Sundays, on every full moon Poya day and on public and bank holidays. It publishes a trading calendar for each year. Because Sri Lanka has many Poya and festival holidays, weeks with four or fewer trading days are common; remember this when counting settlement days.

Market halts

Trading can be paused for the whole market if the S&P SL20 index falls sharply, or for a single share pending an announcement or after unusual price movement. See market halts and circuit breakers.

Practical tips

  1. Prices in the first and last minutes can be volatile; a limit order protects you from surprises.
  2. Plan cash transfers to your broker around settlement and bank cut-off times.
  3. Check the CSE calendar before trades that depend on dates, such as XD dates or rights trading.

Frequently asked questions

Can I place orders before 9:30 am?

Yes. During the pre-open session from 9:00 am you can enter, change and cancel orders. They are matched at the open auction at 9:30 am, then regular trading continues.

When do I get the money from a sale?

Settlement is on the second market day after the trade (T+2). Your broker then pays you according to its own process, which may take additional time depending on how you have asked to be paid.

Is the market open on Poya days?

No. The CSE is closed on weekends, full moon Poya days and other public and bank holidays listed in its annual trading calendar.
