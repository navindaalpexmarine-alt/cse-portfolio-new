Your CDS account explained: how CSE shares are held

When you buy shares on the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) you do not get a paper certificate. The shares are recorded electronically in your account with the Central Depository Systems (Pvt) Ltd (CDS), a subsidiary of the CSE. Your CDS account is the official record of what you own.

Not investment advice. Procedures change; check the CDS website and your broker for current requirements.

Three parties, three roles

WhoRole
YouThe account holder and legal owner of the shares.
Stockbroker (CDS participant)Opens and services the account, places your buy and sell orders, handles your trading cash.
CDSKeeps the official electronic register of who holds which shares and moves them on settlement.

When you trade, the CSE matches your order with another investor's; two market days later (T+2) the CDS moves the shares into the buyer's account and the brokers settle the money.

Your CDS account number

Each account has a number that includes a code for the broker it was opened through. You need it when applying for IPOs, dealing with rights issues and contacting the CDS. Keep it with your other investment records.

Opening an account

Resident individuals can open a CDS account online through the official CSE Mobile App or a broker's web portal, choosing the stockbroker the account will be opened with. Typically you need your NIC, proof of address, bank account details (for example a bank statement or passbook page) and a selfie for identity checks. Joint and corporate accounts and accounts for non-residents have extra requirements; non-resident investors need an Inward Investment Account (IIA) at a bank. Checking what you own

  • CDS statements: the CDS sends periodic statements of holdings and movements.
  • CDS e-Connect: available through the CSE Mobile App and web portal, lets you view holdings and update some personal details (for example adding nominees after the account is opened).
  • Broker platform: your broker's online trading system (such as ATrad) shows holdings in the accounts it services.

If the CDS statement and your own records differ, the usual causes are a corporate action (bonus, split, rights) you did not record or a trade that had not yet settled.

Dividends and your bank details

Companies pay dividends to the bank account recorded with your CDS account. If those details are missing or wrong, payments can be delayed. Update them through your broker or CDS e-Connect whenever you change banks.

Keeping the account safe

  1. Keep your contact details (mobile and email) current so you receive trade and statement notifications.
  2. Read every contract note and statement. Report any trade you did not place to your broker and the CSE immediately.
  3. Never share passwords or one-time codes for your broker platform or the CSE app.
  4. Add a nominee so your heirs can claim shares more easily.
  5. Be wary of anyone offering to "manage" your account using your login. Only licensed investment managers may manage money for others.

Changing broker

You can move shares from one broker's CDS account to an account with another broker by submitting a transfer request through the brokers involved. There is no sale, so no transaction cost, though administrative charges may apply. Your purchase cost stays the same, so carry it across in your own records.

Frequently asked questions

Does my broker own my shares?

No. Shares in your CDS account are registered in your name. The broker is the participant through which the account is operated and through which you trade. If you change broker, your shares can be transferred to a CDS account with the new broker.

Can I have more than one CDS account?

Yes. You can open accounts through different stockbrokers. Each has its own number. Your total holding of a company is the sum across all of them.

What happens to an account I have not used for years?

Long-inactive accounts can be suspended. In 2026 the CDS suspended accounts inactive for more than 15 years after a public notice. If you have an old account, contact your broker or the CDS to check its status and update your details.
